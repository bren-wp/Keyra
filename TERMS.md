## 1. About Keyra

Keyra is an on-device password manager for Android and iOS, developed and published by Brendigo. It is designed for people who wish to manage passwords, private notes and other sensitive information under their control.

## 2. Your data and responsibilities

Your vault is stored locally in encrypted form. You are responsible for choosing a strong master password, safeguarding your device, and securely retaining any backup and Recovery Key needed to restore your data. Losing access to both your credentials and recovery material may make encrypted data permanently inaccessible.

Keyra does not provide a hosted Keyra account or automatic Keyra cloud backup. User-directed exports to third-party document or cloud providers are handled under those providers' own terms.

## 3. Exports and document reports

Encrypted `.keyra` backups are different from PDF overview reports. A PDF overview ZIP contains only aggregate item counts, without passwords, personal item titles, usernames, TOTP seeds or recovery secrets. **The PDF/ZIP report is not encrypted and cannot restore a vault.** Review the file destination before sharing it.
